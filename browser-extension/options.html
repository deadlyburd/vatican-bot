ℹ️ About This Extension

Vatican Ticket Monitor helps you find available Vatican Museums tickets by automatically checking the official website at your specified intervals.

How it works:
1. Select your desired date and number of visitors
2. Click "Start Monitoring"
3. The extension checks availability in the background
4. You'll receive a notification when tickets become available

Privacy: All data is stored locally on your device. No information is sent to external servers.
